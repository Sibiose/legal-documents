# Privacy Policy — In My Prayers

**Effective date:** 10.10.2026
**Last updated:** 10.10.2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the **In My Prayers** mobile app (the "app") handles
information. The app is made by Maris Corneliu ("I", "me", "we", "us"). If you have any
question about this policy, contact corneliumaris.apps@gmail.com.

## The short version

- **Everything you write stays on your phone.** Your prayers, the people and categories you
  add, your notes, answers and reminders are saved only in a local database on your device.
- **We have no servers and no accounts.** There is nothing to sign up for, and nothing you
  write is ever sent to us or to anyone else by the app.
- **No ads, no analytics, no tracking.** The app contains no advertising, analytics,
  tracking, profiling or crash-reporting tools, and it does not use an advertising ID.
- **We cannot see, read, recover or delete your data**, because we never receive it.
- **Two things outside our control can hold a copy or details of your use:** Android's own
  backup feature (if it is turned on for your Google account) and Google Play (which
  distributes the app). Both are described below.

## 1. Who is responsible

The app is developed and published by Maris Corneliu, Arad, Romania
(contact: corneliumaris.apps@gmail.com).

Because the app does not send your content to us, we do not collect or process the
personal data you type into the app. You are the one who decides what to store, and it is
stored only on your device. The only personal data we may receive is what you choose to
send us yourself, for example if you email us (see section 9).

## 2. What the app stores on your device

When you use the app, it saves the following in a database inside the app's private
storage on your device:

| What | Examples | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Prayers** | Title, optional details, person, category, schedule (daily, weekdays, one date, or none), status (ongoing or closed), creation date, how many times you prayed | To show your prayer list and today's prayers |
| **People** | Names you enter and the color you pick | To group prayers by person |
| **Categories** | Category names and colors | To group prayers by category |
| **Prayer log** | The date and time each time you mark a prayer as prayed | To show today's progress and your history |
| **Answers and updates** | The text you write and the date you choose | To keep the prayer's timeline and your praise log |
| **Reminders** | Reminder times and days, on or off | To schedule notifications on your device |
| **Preferences** | Theme (light/dark/system), accent color, language, whether onboarding is done, haptics on or off | To remember your settings |

This information never leaves your device through the app. It is not shared with us, sold,
or used for advertising.

### Sensitive information

Prayers can be very personal. What you write may reveal your religious beliefs, your
health or someone else's, family matters, or other sensitive details about you and about
the people you pray for. Because all of it stays on your device, we never process it.
Please keep in mind:

- **Anyone who can unlock your phone can open the app and read it.** The app has no
  separate password or lock. We recommend protecting your device with a screen lock.
- **The app does not add its own encryption** on top of the protection your device already
  provides. Modern Android devices encrypt their storage when a screen lock is set.
- **Information about other people** (for example a friend's name and illness) is yours to
  keep for your own private, personal use. Please be thoughtful about what you write about
  others.

## 3. What the app does not do

The app does **not**:

- create accounts or ask for your email, phone number or name;
- connect to any server operated by us, or send your content anywhere;
- contain advertising, analytics, attribution, tracking or crash-reporting software;
- use your advertising ID or build a profile of you;
- access your location, contacts, calendar, camera, microphone, photos or files;
- sell, rent or share personal information with anyone;
- use your content to train artificial intelligence or for any other purpose.

The app works fully offline.

## 4. Notifications

If you turn on reminders ("nudges" or reminders on a prayer), the app asks for permission to
show notifications. Reminders are scheduled **locally on your device** by Android. They are
not sent through any server, and the app does not register for push notifications.

Please note:

- **A reminder for a specific prayer shows that prayer's title** in the notification.
  Depending on your phone's settings, notifications can appear on the lock screen, where
  others may see them. You can hide sensitive notification content in your phone's settings
  (usually *Settings → Notifications → Notifications on lock screen*), or turn off
  notifications for the app entirely.
- General nudges show a generic message ("Time to pray") and no prayer content.
- You can withdraw notification permission at any time in your phone's settings. The rest of
  the app keeps working.

## 5. Device backups and transfers

The app allows Android's built-in backup. If **Backup by Google One** (or your device's
backup feature) is turned on, Android may include a copy of the app's data — including your
prayers and the names you entered — in your device backup, stored in **your own Google
account**. The same data can be copied when you move to a new phone using Android's transfer
tools.

These backups are created and managed by Google and your device, not by us. We cannot access
them. They are governed by Google's terms and privacy policy
(<https://policies.google.com/privacy>). If you do not want the app's data to be backed up,
you can turn off backup in your phone's settings (usually *Settings → Google → Backup*).

## 6. Google Play

You download the app from Google Play, which is operated by Google. Google collects
information about your device, your account and your use of Google Play under its own
privacy policy (<https://policies.google.com/privacy>). We do not control this.

Through the Google Play Console, we can see **aggregated statistics** (such as the number
of installs, device models, Android versions and countries) and, if you have allowed your
device to share usage and diagnostics with Google, **crash and performance reports**.
These reports describe technical problems (for example the device model, Android version,
app version and the technical details of a crash). They do not contain your prayers, names or
other content. We use them only to fix bugs and keep the app working.

## 7. Links to websites

The app's Settings screen contains links to this Privacy Policy and to the Terms of Use. When
you tap a link, it opens in your web browser, which (along with the website you visit) handles
that request under its own privacy practices.

## 8. Permissions the app uses

| Permission | Why it is needed |
|---|---|
| **Notifications** | To show the reminders you set up. Asked only when you turn reminders on. |
| **Run at startup** | To re-schedule your reminders after the phone restarts. |

## 9. If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and whatever
you write. We use this only to reply to you and help with your question. Our legal basis is
our legitimate interest in answering messages sent to us (and, where it applies, taking
steps you request). We keep the conversation only as long as needed to deal with it, and
then delete it, unless we must keep it for legal reasons. We do not use it for marketing.

## 10. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it:

- **Delete individual items in the app.** Deleting a prayer also deletes its prayer log,
  answers and reminders. Deleting a person or a category keeps their prayers, which are just
  no longer linked to them.
- **Delete everything:** clear the app's storage (*Settings → Apps → In My Prayers →
  Storage → Clear storage*) or uninstall the app. This permanently removes all data from the
  device.
- **Backups:** copies in your Google backup are kept and deleted according to Google's
  settings and policies.

Because we have no copy of your data, **we cannot recover it** if it is deleted or lost
(for example if your phone is lost, reset or damaged and there is no backup).

## 11. Your rights

If you are in the European Union, the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, data
protection law (such as the GDPR) gives you rights over your personal data, including the
rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to its processing, and to data
portability.

- **For data in the app:** we do not hold it, so we cannot access, correct or delete it for
  you — but you can do all of this yourself at any time in the app or in your phone's
  settings, as described above.
- **For emails you send us:** you can ask us to access or delete them at corneliumaris.apps@gmail.com .
- **For Google Play and Google backups:** contact Google, which is responsible for that data.

We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising or profiling.

## 12. Children

The app is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal data
from anyone — children included. Because the app does not send any information to us, no
personal data about children reaches us through the app. If you believe a child has sent
us personal data by email, contact us and we will delete it.

## 13. Security

Your data is protected by your device's own security: Android keeps each app's private
storage separate from other apps, and modern devices encrypt their storage when a screen lock
is set. No method of storage is completely secure, so please use a screen lock and keep your
device's software up to date.

## 14. International transfers

We do not transfer your data anywhere, because we do not receive it. Google may process data
for Google Play and Google backups in various countries under its own safeguards.
